# By hand: WP 2.3, Review mode and the history panel

2026-09-09. Design 4.4 end to end against the installed release build —
`pnpm tauri build --bundles app`, copied to `/Applications/MD Reader.app`
— driven with real keystrokes and clicks, with window screenshots at
every step. Not `tauri dev`, and not a test harness.

The document was a 29-line migration plan with a heading tree, a
paragraph per section, a three-row table, a bullet list, and a short
section at the end. The "agent" was a shell heredoc rewriting the file
under the open window.

## What the agent did

Six things at once, chosen so that every kind of change is in the same
write: it rewrapped Background to a narrower measure, changed the last
three words of Scope, moved the whole Risks section above Tasks, added a
fourth bullet to it, changed one cell of the table (`Lee` to `Ash`), and
deleted the "Out of scope" section.

## The marks

The status bar said `plan.md changed on disk · 5 changes merged in` and
the badge said `Changes 5`.

- **Background: nothing.** The rewrap is invisible, which is the case
  design 7.3 exists for.
- **Scope: one amber bar** beside the changed paragraph.
- **Risks: one blue bar** down the heading and its three original
  bullets — one mark for one movement, not four.
- **The new bullet: green**, its own bar inside the blue run.
- **The table: an amber bar down its whole height.** This is the hole
  WP 2.2 left open and this work package closed: the table is a live
  preview widget, no line under it is drawn, and until now the change
  was counted and stepped to and marked nowhere. The bar covers the
  widget rather than the row, because the widget is the finest thing the
  view knows about there.
- **A red notch under the table**, where the deleted section had been.

## Review mode

`⇧⌘R`. The status line said `Reviewing changes` and five panels appeared:

- `CHANGED` over Scope, reading `… reader and leave the writer ~~alone.~~
  for a later change.` — the untouched opening elided to a `…`, the old
  words struck through, the new ones on a green ground.
- `MOVED HERE` over the Risks heading, with a Revert button and no body:
  nothing textual changed.
- `ADDED` over the new bullet, with no body either. The added text is in
  the buffer a line below the panel and saying it twice is noise.
- `CHANGED` over the table, reading `| Bench harness | ~~Lee~~ Ash |
  19 Oct |`. This is the answer to the granularity the bar gives up.
- `REMOVED` at the end, holding both deleted blocks struck through:
  `## Out of scope` and `Anything to do with the network layer.`

`⌥⌘G` stepped between them and the panel under the cursor took a
stronger tint, so the walk is visible.

## Reverting

Pressed **Revert** on the `REMOVED` panel. The section came back where
it had been, with a proper blank line before it, the panel went, the
notch went, the count dropped to 4 and the word count rose from 94 to
104. Nothing else moved.

Stepped to the table change and pressed **`⌥⌘Z`**. The row went back to
`| Bench harness | Lee | 19 Oct |`. Checked the file on disk afterwards:
the pipes are byte-identical to the original, the restored section has
exactly one blank line before it, and the file is 30 lines with no stray
whitespace anywhere.

Autosave wrote both reverts a second later, which is the rule everything
else in the app follows and is worth knowing: a revert reaches the disk
before you have finished looking at it, and `⌘Z` is the way back.

## The history panel

`⇧⌘B`, then **HISTORY**. Five versions, newest first, each a time, a
hand and a size:

```
02:17 PM  Autosave  674 B
02:17 PM  Outside   617 B
02:16 PM  You       614 B
02:09 PM  Outside   617 B
02:08 PM  You       614 B
```

**Comparing.** Pressed Mark Reviewed first, so the badge read nothing at
all, then clicked the 02:08 PM row. The three outstanding changes came
straight back: the row took a blue edge, the panel grew a
`Comparing · show changes since you last looked` bar, the status bar
grew a `Comparing` button, and the message said `Marks show what changed
since 02:08 PM`. That is the whole point of the panel — the gutter's
question asked about any moment the app has kept.

**Opening a version.** Pressed **Open** on the 02:17 PM outside write.
It opened as its own tab, `plan.md at 02:17 PM`, in Source mode, with
the panels marking what it changed relative to the 02:08 PM version that
was selected — `plan.md: 02:08 PM to 02:17 PM` in the status bar. The
history panel said `Save this file to start keeping its versions`, which
is true of it: it has no file.

**Restoring.** Pressed **Restore** on the 02:17 PM outside write. The
buffer became that version, the marks recomputed against the comparison,
and the status said `Restored the version from 02:17 PM`. One `⌘Z` took
the whole thing back.

The version that was in the buffer was *not* added to the history as a
new row — because it was already there. The protective snapshot the
restore takes first is deduplicated against the newest one for the path,
and the autosave a second earlier had already stored exactly those
bytes. Nothing was lost, which is what design 4.4 asks for; the row is
labelled Autosave rather than You.

## Session

`⌘Q` and relaunched. Seven tabs came back, not eight: the comparison
view was left out, as a view of a past version should be. The sidebar
came back on the History panel with the list populated. Review mode did
not come back, and the comparison base did not either — both are about a
set of changes in front of you, and a restart has none.

## Left alone

Nothing needed recovering. The app was left with the sample tab closed
and the sidebar shut, which is how it was found.
