Allowed: marked, 2, nd, underlined, struck, Cmd, a break
here, and red.

Refused: <script>alert(1)</script>, <iframe src="x"></iframe>, <style>b{}</style>, <b onclick="steal()">handler</b>, <div>a block tag inline</div>, <a href="x">a link</a>.

A span with more than a colour is text: <span style="color:red;font-size:99px">too much</span>. A span with a colour that is not one: <span style="color:url(x)">not a colour</span>.

Nesting: outer inner outer. Crossed tags: one <sup>two three</sup>. Unclosed: <mark>runs to the end of the paragraph.
